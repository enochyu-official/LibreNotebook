# Contributing to LibreNotebook

First and foremost, thank you so much for your interest and taking
your time to contribute! All contributions are welcomed and encouraged
with minor details below. To contribute, please either open an issue
or submit a pull request.

* Type of Contribution: Really any contributions ranging from a typo to
  writing a new chapter is welcomed!
* AI Use
  * If your content is AI generated, please disclose it. We actually
    encourage the use of AI for assistance! However, we value
    transparency, credits, and quality. Unlike AI assisted contents,
    many AI generated contents lack proper credits and quality checks.
    We will not nor have the capacity to check if any contents are mostly
    AI generated. However, we highly encourage disclosing for transparency
    of contributors. Below are examples of different AI usage.
  * Examples of AI Assisted contents includes, but are not limited to:
    * Spell checks for original contents
    * Checking whether the content is correct
    * Debugging LaTeX compilation and Tikz errors
  * Examples of AI Generated contents include, but are not limited to:
    * Using AI to generate contents and paraphrasing afterwards
    * Using AI as a "search engine" as a sole way to find contents
      like definitions and theorems to include in the notes
    * Using AI to rewrite a large portion of the original contents
* Please be kind and welcoming :))

Thank you for your time and efforts!
