LibreNotebook

A Giant Notebook

LibreNotebook is a personal notebook that I write as I explore different topics of my interest. I personally learn the best when I explain the contents, so I naturally write my notes as if I am explaining the topics to other people. Although LibreNotebook reflects my fields of interest, I strive to include thorough insights whenever possible.

Having said that, LibreNotebook is not meant to replace textbooks nor dedicated resources. Rather, it is intended to encourage and accelerate open education by returning the knowledge and skills that I received from public resources. For anyone interested in the topics outlined below, I hope LibreNotebook serve as a useful resource.
