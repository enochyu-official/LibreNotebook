Modular Arithmetic

The two major topics from olympiad Number Theory should be Diophantine equations and modular arithmetic. Having two major parts, there are many contents and techniques to discuss in modular arithmetic just as we did with Diophantine equations. In this note, we will discuss more fundamental topics like primitive roots and quadratic residue and will further discuss different techniques used in problems.
