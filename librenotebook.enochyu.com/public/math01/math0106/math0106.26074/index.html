0.1.1. 2000 APMO Problem 1 (\( \bullet \bullet \circ \))
 Find \(S = \sum _{i=0}^{101} \frac {x_i^3}{1 - 3x_i + 3x_i^2}\) for \(x_i = \frac {i}{101}\).
